## Removed and Flagged Problematic Citations

25. **Asensio et al. (2017)**
    - *Original Claim:* "Changes in noise levels in the city of Madrid after the modification of flight paths", *Transportation Research Part D*, 57, 107–116.
    - *Audit Finding:* **UNVERIFIABLE / DISCREDITED.** Volume 57 of TR Part D contains no paper by Asensio et al. The closest verified publication is Asensio, Pavón, & de Arcas (2020), "Acoustic effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on Madrid", *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* 148(3), 1748–1755, which analyzes lockdown noise reductions, not flight path modifications.
    - *Action:* **Permanently removed.** Replaced with verified citations on flight track concentration and community annoyance: Brenner & Hansman (2017) and GAO (2021).

    - *Action:* **Permanently removed.** Environmental justice discussion reframed around documented GAO (2021) and FAA regulatory findings.
